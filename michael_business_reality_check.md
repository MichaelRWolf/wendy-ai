# Business Reality Check: Revenue or Hobby

## The Core Dilemma

I love her to the moon as a partner. I also cannot tell her hard truths without risking a relationship rift or triggering her self-invalidation.

She's a seedling that needs time. But it's been 5-15 years at approximately $0 revenue.

She's willful and doesn't want to be told what to do. I'm know-it-all and want to tell her what to do. We're both working on ourselves and don't need anyone else poking at those wounds. But the world doesn't care about vulnerability.

**The reality:** If they don't want it, they don't want it. That's not mean. That's how markets work.

## The Years-Long Pattern

Countless workshops comp'ed in with agreements for work. None have delivered.

Bootstrapping is one thing. This has moved to delusion and hope, wrapped in an honest desire to make it a business.

**I don't see a valid business plan.** Or business goal. Not that I can do better. But I can see it doesn't have a plan that would attract real $$$, so I don't expect it to return real $$.

## The Skills Gap

She can bring value. She just can't attract or find customers. And she thinks the two of us can.

I don't.

What this business needs: promotion, marketing, and honesty about creating packages people will pay real cash for.

Neither of us has those strengths. (Me: technical. Her: spiritual/leadership. Neither: sales/marketing.)

**Bad form to expect gophers to fly or birds to chop wood.** She has her strengths. Ditto me. But we're trying to do the work we're not good at, instead of hiring someone who is.

## The Calendar Inversion Problem

Right now: "Next summer I want to lead a 6-month course."

Reality check: "Winter and Spring need to create funnel for summer. Fall funnels that. Summer--NOW, fucking now--there will be revenue that proves any of this is even worth talking about."

**If the mini-offer cannot sell next week or next month, it is a waste of time (but maybe fun) to plan for:**

- A 3rd book in a series
- A 6-month course
- More than 0 attendees at $0/attendee revenue
- A year of investment toward a too-long-from-now goal

## The Pivot Required

I do not want to pivot from her personal focus on polishing material only to have the two of us polish some technical piece or delivery mechanism.

If UTMs and better copy flip a sale, great. But if not, then pretending they will or getting lost in the fun of learning something new is missing the piece: **We would not have the cash flow to pay someone to do this work, so we should not be doing it for free.**

There's a point at which bootstrapping a zero cash flow period becomes infinite waiting for cash flow.

I need to call that point.

## The Risk and the Hope

Doubling down like this could be the crucible and lens needed to get honest. Or it could crumble.

Maybe this recent switch--toward actual revenue testing--is different. But I need to see evidence.

Until then, I'd rather stay out of a $0 return business than risk getting in the way of her knowing how it should be and me doing it wrong.
