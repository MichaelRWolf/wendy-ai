# Request for Services: Build Revenue Through Tested Offerings

## From Wendy to Michael

Michael, I need your help. But not the way we've tried before.

For years, I've built content, frameworks, and community. I've invested in understanding my people, my voice, and what I want to offer. And that's been real and necessary.

But I'm also aware--and you've helped me see this--that I have never actually asked my market to pay me. I've created, refined, gathered people around me, and assumed the value would somehow translate to sustainable income. It hasn't.

**I'm ready to test that assumption.** Not to abandon the work I love. But to build *structure* around it that actually generates revenue.

I'm asking you to help me run three tight experiments--each with a clear financial goal, a short timeframe, and a hard decision point. No infinite refinement. No planning a 6-month course until I know someone will buy a 4-week pilot.

---

## What I'm Proposing: The 30-Day Revenue Rhythm

I want to steal from the language you and I both love--the idea of a gentle, repeating cycle. But make it about testing offers in the real market, not just polishing them in my head.

**The principle:** Small tests, fast feedback, real money. If it works, we build on it. If it doesn't, we learn what to change and try again. In weeks, not years.

---

## Phase 1: The Mini-Offer Test (30 Days)

### The Hypothesis

*Can I sell a 4-week THRIVE pilot at $X per seat?*

This isn't a free event. This isn't a "register and see what happens." This is: **Will someone pay real money for this right now?**

### What Needs to Happen (My Asks of You)

1. **Define the offer together:**
   - Name: (e.g., "THRIVE 4-Week Pilot")
   - Price: $X (what makes sense to you?)
   - What they get: 4 weekly sessions + materials + email support
   - Why now: Why would someone buy this in the next 2 weeks vs. waiting for "the real thing"?

2. **Create the sales message:**
   - A short invitation (2-3 paragraphs) that tells the truth about what THRIVE is
   - Speak to the consciousness registers if that resonates; speak to real life challenges otherwise
   - Make it inviting, not desperate
   - Test whether the *message* sells, not whether the *design* is perfect

3. **Set up basic tracking** (not fancy, but real):
   - Facebook ad → landing page → registration → payment
   - Track: How many saw it? How many clicked? How many registered? How many paid?
   - Add UTM tracking so we know where people came from

4. **Run the experiment:**
   - Spend $50-150 over 30 days
   - Target people who care about the things I care about (consciousness, spiritual growth, real life resilience)
   - Send them to a page that asks them to pay, not just "register"

### The Measurements (Not Vanity Metrics)

| What                                 | Why It Matters                       | Success Looks Like               |
|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| **Ad Spend**                         | Accountability                       | $50-150 (not more without a win) |
| **People Who Click**                 | They were interested enough to click | 100-300 clicks                   |
| **People Who Land on Page**          | They got to the actual offer         | 80-200 visitors                  |
| **People Who Attempt Registration**  | They wanted to buy                   | 15-40 started the form           |
| **People Who Complete Registration** | They said yes                        | 10-30 completed                  |
| **People Who Attend**                | They kept their commitment           | >60% of enrolled                 |
| **People Who Pay**                   | **This is the real metric**          | ≥1-3 people pay $X               |
| **Total Revenue**                    | Did we beat the ad spend?            | Revenue > $50 spent              |
| **Cost Per Sale**                    | Is this sustainable?                 | <$150 per paying customer        |

### The Pass/Fail Decision (The Hard Part)

**If by [end of month] we have:**

- ✅ 1-3 people who paid for the pilot AND
- ✅ Total revenue > ad spend
- ✅ Click-through rate > 1%
- ✅ Registration completion rate > 50%

**Then:** We move to Phase 2. The message works. The offer works. There's demand.

**If:**

- ❌ Zero people enroll and pay, OR
- ❌ Total revenue < ad spend, OR
- ❌ <3 registrations from $100+ spend

**Then:** We stop and ask: **What do we change?**

- Is the price wrong?
- Is the message wrong?
- Is the timing wrong?
- Is the consciousness register framing not resonating?
- Are we reaching the wrong people?

We pick ONE thing to change, spend another $50 to test it, and try again. Fast.

We do NOT move to Phase 2. We do NOT plan the 6-week course. We do NOT assume "it will work better with a bigger budget or better design."

## Phase 2: Medium Offer (If Phase 1 Works)

*Only if Phase 1 passes the gate.*

Hypothesis: Can we sell an 8-week deeper program?

Same structure: clear offer, real price, fast timeline, clear metrics, pass/fail decision.

| What                              | Threshold                            |
|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Spend**                         | $200-500                             |
| **Mini-Offer Sales (cumulative)** | ≥5 people across all ads             |
| **Mini-Offer Revenue**            | ≥$1,000 total                        |
| **Cost Per Sale**                 | <$200                                |
| **Interest in Next Level**        | ≥1-2 people ask about 8-week program |

**Pass:** Medium-offer exists. Pilot customers want more. We build it.

**Fail:** Customers are happy but not progressing. Price is wrong. Offer is wrong. We pause and reconsider.

---

## Phase 3: The Big Vision (If Phases 1 & 2 Work)

*Only AFTER we've proven:*

- People will pay for pilot
- People will pay for medium level
- Revenue > acquisition cost

Then and only then do I plan the 6-month retreat, the 2-year curriculum, the book series.

Until then, those are dreams. Not business projects.

---

## What I'm NOT Asking You To Do

- Polish my brand
- Build my website
- Create 50 pieces of content
- Automate workflows
- Teach me marketing theory
- Help me write a business plan that looks good on paper

## What I AM Asking

- Help me test if the market wants what I'm offering
- Set up tracking so we know what actually happened
- Make decisions with real data, not hope
- Pivot fast when something doesn't work
- Stop spending time when it's not working
- Build the big vision only after the small test proves it's worth building

---

## The Timeline

- **Week 1:** Define offer, write message, set up tracking
- **Week 2-4:** Run ads, measure, gather data
- **Week 4 end:** Decide: Phase 2 or Pivot?
- **If Phase 2:** Repeat structure for 4-6 weeks
- **If Success:** THEN plan bigger offerings

**Total time before big commitments:** 8-12 weeks maximum

Not years. Not "after the next course is polished." Not "once the retreatcenter is designed."

## What Draws Me to This Approach

You've said (and I hear it): I've invested 5-15 years and generated nearly $0 in revenue. That's data. It says something.

You've also said: The problem isn't the content or the frameworks. The problem is nobody knows about it, and nobody's asked to pay.

Both are true.

**I don't want to keep refining something nobody's buying.** I want to know if there's actually a market for this. Not someday. Now.

If there is: We build on real demand, real revenue, real customers.

If there isn't: I'll stop pretending it's a business and enjoy it for what it is. A beautiful life, a spiritual path, a way I'm in community with people I care about. That's enough.

But I can't know until I test.

## What I Need from You

Your willingness to:

1. Help me define an offer and price it
2. Help me track whether people buy it
3. Make hard decisions based on data, not hope
4. Say "this didn't work, let's try something else" or "this worked, let's build on it"
5. Protect both of us from spending time on something that's proven it won't generate revenue

I know you're not a marketer. Neither am I. But you know how to measure things and make decisions. I know what I want to offer and why it matters.

Together, maybe we can find out if anyone wants to buy it.

## The One Question That Matters

If we run Phase 1 for 30 days and nobody enrolls in the paid pilot, what happens next?

Do we:

- Try Phase 1 again with different messaging/targeting/price? (Fast learning)
- Build a bigger course and hope that sells better? (Infinite hope)
- Admit that right now, at this moment, there isn't a viable market? (Hard truth)

I need to know your answer to this before we start. Because it defines whether we're running a business experiment or playing at business while hoping for a different outcome.

I'm ready for the experiment. I'm not sure I'm ready for the hard truth, but I want to be.

Will you help?
